    Glossary and Source

    Necessity: strictly necessary
    This type of cookie is essential for you to browse the website and use its features, this website uses it to record your cookie setting preferences. You can read more about this at What are the exceptions — Information Commissioner's Office.
    Necessity: functionality
    This type of cookie is used to support some functionality, without it some functions will not be available. See what will be unavailable by mouseover/focus the icon on the Privacy Settings Panel in the lower left corner of the screen.
    Persistence: persistent
    This type of cookie is stored on your device for a duration, closing the browser will not delete it.
    Provenance: first-party
    This type of cookie is stored on your device by the website you are visiting, namely this website.
    Duration
    The time that cookies are stored on your device. They will be automatically deleted after they expire.
    Source
    To make our privacy policy clear and easy to understand, this website uses a modified cookie classification. You can view the unmodified and other unused cookie classifications here: Cookies, the GDPR, and the ePrivacy Directive — GDPR.eu.
